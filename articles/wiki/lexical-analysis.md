# The Lexical Analysis: EO's Preliminary Empirical Grounding

---

**Epistemic status:** Empirically strong on proportionality and face-level clustering; mixed on axis independence; coordinate geometry predictions not met.

---

## **Overview**

This study tests whether the three axes of EO's capacity ground — Mode (α), Domain (η), and Object (Ω) — correspond to real, independent dimensions in the semantic geometry of natural language. The test instrument is clause-level annotation across a multilingual corpus, with embeddings generated by a model that has never seen EO. No EO vocabulary appears in any embedded text.

**Corpus:** 19,764 clauses embedded; 9,221 consensus clauses across 41 languages. **Annotation models:** Claude and GPT-4o, run independently.

---

## **Design**

Each clause was sent to both models with the following prompt verbatim. No EO vocabulary appears anywhere in it.

**System instruction:**

> You are a linguist analyzing clauses. For each clause you will answer three short questions about the kind of transformation the clause describes. Answer each question with exactly one of the options listed. Do not add explanation unless asked. Return your answer as JSON: `{"q1": "...", "q2": "...", "q3": "..."}`

**Classification prompt:**

> **Q1 — How is the transformation structured?** SEPARATING — the clause is primarily about dividing, distinguishing, analyzing, or drawing something apart CONNECTING — the clause is primarily about linking, bridging, relating, or holding things together PRODUCING — the clause is primarily about making, generating, creating, or causing something to happen
> 
> **Q2 — What level of reality is being transformed?** EXISTENCE — whether something is: presence, absence, coming into being, disappearing ORGANIZATION — how things are arranged: structure, boundaries, relations, composition MEANING — what something signifies: interpretation, value, perspective, what something registers as
> 
> **Q3 — What kind of thing is being acted on?** BACKGROUND — an ambient condition, an environment, a field or substrate, the context something happens within PARTICULAR — a specific individual thing: this named object, this event, this person PATTERN — a recurring regularity: a rule, a type, a schema, something that holds across many instances

**Answer mapping to EO axis values:**

| **Q1 plain** | **→ EO** | **Q2 plain** | **→ EO** | **Q3 plain** | **→ EO** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| SEPARATING | DIFFERENTIATING | EXISTENCE | EXISTENCE | BACKGROUND | CONDITION |
| CONNECTING | RELATING | ORGANIZATION | STRUCTURE | PARTICULAR | ENTITY |
| PRODUCING | GENERATING | MEANING | SIGNIFICANCE | PATTERN | PATTERN |

The EO translation is applied only after classification. The classifying models see only the plain-language options.

**Pre-committed predictions (locked before processing):**

1. Distance scales monotonically with number of differing axes
2. All pairwise ARI < 0.10 (axes independent)
3. Q2 z-score exceeds Q1 in 20+ languages
4. Inter-model kappa > 0.50 on Q1, > 0.40 on Q2, > 0.35 on Q3

### **Axis Independence (ARI)**

Adjusted Rand Index between axis label pairs. Prediction: all < 0.10.

| **Pair** | **ARI** | **Status** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Q1 / Q2 | +0.185 | borderline |
| Q1 / Q3 | +0.028 | independent ✓ |
| Q2 / Q3 | −0.008 | independent ✓ |

**Pre-committed prediction 2: failed.** Q1/Q2 exceeds the 0.10 threshold.

The Q1/Q2 correlation is stable across both model runs and all label sets. A cell-exclusion test excluding sparse and dominant cells (RELATING/STRUCTURE; 2,889 clauses removed) raised the ARI from 0.185 to 0.268 rather than lowering it, providing no support for the hypothesis that the correlation is an artifact of helix-predicted sparsity. The exclusion test cannot cleanly isolate structural dependency from label-diversity reduction, so neither reading is definitively confirmed.

### **Helix Dependency Tests**

**Test 1 — Directional Asymmetry**

H(Q2|Q1) = 1.303 bits; H(Q1|Q2) = 1.169 bits. Asymmetry = +0.134 bits in the Mode→Domain direction. Permutation p = 0.357. Not significant at conventional thresholds.

**Test 2 — Mode Ordinal Predicts Domain Ordinal**

Spearman r = −0.420, p = 0.000 (n = 8,000). The ordinal position of Mode predicts the ordinal complexity of Domain, with a negative correlation: lower Mode ordinal co-occurs with higher Domain ordinal. Significant.

---

### **Coordinate Geometry (α/η/Ω Metric Test)**

EO's axis coordinate systems predict specific ratios of inter-centroid distances in embedding space. Results across both CONSIGSUS and COMBINED sets:

**Mode α {0,1,2} — prediction: equal steps (D↔R ≈ R↔G)** Step ratio observed: ~1.50. Not met.

**Domain η {−1, +1, √2} — prediction: E↔S / S↔Sig ≈ 4.8** Observed ratio: ~0.58–0.64. Not met. Direction of the asymmetry is reversed from prediction.

**Object Ω {√2, 2, 2^√2} — prediction: C↔P ≈ C↔E + E↔P (additivity)** Observed: non-additive. Not met.

None of the three coordinate geometry predictions are supported.

## **What Was Not Found**

- Coordinate geometry predictions (axis-specific distance ratios) are not supported in embedding space.
- Helix directional asymmetry (information-flow test) is not significant at p < 0.05.
- Unsupervised clustering (KMeans) does not spontaneously reproduce EO axis structure; this is expected given that embedding space is organized primarily by semantic content.
- Q1/Q2 independence (ARI < 0.10) does not hold.
