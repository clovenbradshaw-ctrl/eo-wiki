# The 27 Forms

The operator encodes Mode × Domain. The Object class appears as a coordinate badge: **-** (Condition) · **+** (Figure) · ***** (Pattern) — "Figure" per the shipped `GRAINS` naming (`native/kernel/cube.js`); an earlier revision of this article called this position "Entity," which collided with the unrelated terrain name Entity (`TERRAIN_BY_DOMAIN.Existence.Figure`) and disagreed with [The EO capacity ground](/the-eo-phase-space-cube)'s "Particular." Each position is fully specified by either `OPERATOR(Object)` or its symbol, and carries a canonical coordinate address (Mode, Domain, Object).

**Axis coordinates:**

1. Mode: 0 (Differentiating) · 1 (Relating) · 2 (Generating)
2. Domain: −1 (Existence) · +1 (Structure) · √2 (Significance — named "Interpretation" in the current `eoreader7` implementation, `native/kernel/cube.js` `DOMAINS`)
3. Object: 2 (Condition) · √2 (Figure) · 2^√2 (Pattern)

## What Each Position Means

**NUL — marking absence**

1. ∅- (0, −1, 2) — a field has cleared; a background state is gone
2. ∅+ (0, −1, √2) — a specific thing is not present; classical negation
3. ∅* (0, −1, 2^√2) — a regularity has dissolved; a structural order has ceased

**SIG — marking presence**

1. ○- (1, −1, 2) — a background state has become detectable; a field is active
2. ○+ (1, −1, √2) — a specific thing is registered as present; recognition
3. ○* (1, −1, 2^√2) — a regularity is in effect; a structure is operative

**INS — instantiating**

1. ●- (2, −1, 2) — a background is established; a field is opened
2. ●+ (2, −1, √2) — a specific thing is brought into being; creation
3. ●* (2, −1, 2^√2) — a regularity is established; a structure is made to recur

**SEG — segmenting**

1. ｜- (0, +1, 2) — an ambient field is partitioned into regions
2. ｜+ (0, +1, √2) — a bounded thing is divided into distinguishable parts
3. ｜* (0, +1, 2^√2) — sub-patterns are distinguished within a larger regularity

**CON — connecting**

1. ⋈- (1, +1, 2) — ambient fields are linked; background states placed in relation
2. ⋈+ (1, +1, √2) — specific things are related to each other
3. ⋈* (1, +1, 2^√2) — regularities are related; structural correspondences established

**SYN — synthesizing**

1. △- (2, +1, 2) — connected conditions produce a new emergent background state
2. △+ (2, +1, √2) — connected entities produce a new composite whole
3. △* (2, +1, 2^√2) — connected patterns produce a higher-order regularity

**DEF — defining**

1. ⊢- (0, √2, 2) — this background state is marked as distinct from that one
2. ⊢+ (0, √2, √2) — this entity carries a different meaning from that one
3. ⊢* (0, √2, 2^√2) — this paradigm is marked as distinct from that one

**EVA — evaluating**

1. ⊨- (1, √2, 2) — competing background states are judged; a criterion selects among them
2. ⊨+ (1, √2, √2) — an entity's competing interpretations are weighed and a judgment is rendered
3. ⊨* (1, √2, 2^√2) — rival paradigms are tested against one another

**REC — recursing**

1. ⊛- (2, √2, 2) — a condition takes itself as its own ground; recursive ambient self-reference
2. ⊛+ (2, √2, √2) — an entity models itself; reflexive self-awareness
3. ⊛* (2, √2, 2^√2) — a pattern encompasses its own patterning; recursive systemic self-organization

## Note on SEG Symbol

SEG's symbol `|` is the markdown table column delimiter and will render as a blank cell in table contexts. The canonical glyph `｜` (U+FF5C, fullwidth vertical line) is used throughout, distinct from the ASCII pipe `|` (U+007C). In non-table prose, `|` is fine.
