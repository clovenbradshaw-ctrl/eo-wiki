*A working glossary of EO's terms. It is deliberately thin: each entry gives the short form and points to the article that owns the term. By the wiki's own account of [definition](/identity-and-definition), a definition is only as authoritative as its giver, so every entry names its giver. Where an article and this page disagree, the article wins. The older glossary in the [EO Handbook](/eo-handbook) (Appendix G) predates several reconciliations; where they differ, this page follows the current articles.*

## The framework

**EO.** Existential, Emergent, and Experiential Ontology. The three expansions are interchangeable by design and are not to be flattened into one. See [Experiential Ontology](/emergent-ontology-eo).

**Capacity ground.** EO's three-axis, 27-cell phase space (3 × 3 × 3). Its axes are Mode, Domain, and Object. See [The Three Dimensions of EO](/the-three-dimensions-of-eo) and [The EO Phase Space Cube](/the-eo-phase-space-cube).

**Mode** (Identity). The axis of directionality: Differentiating, Relating, Generating. *What is this becoming?*

**Domain** (Space). The axis of arena: Existence, Structure, Significance (called Interpretation in some articles). *Where does this occur?*

**Object** (Time). The axis of target: Ground, Figure, Pattern. Not clock-time but the temporal character of what is acted on. *What kind of thing is being acted on?* See [The Three Faces of EO](/the-three-faces-of-emergent-ontology).

**Face.** A 3 × 3 projection of the 27-cell structure, made by crossing two axes. There are three: the **Act face** (Mode × Domain: the nine operators, *what are you doing*), the **Site face** (Domain × Object: nine terrains, *where are you*), and the **Stance** or **Resolution face** (Mode × Object: nine stances, *how are you holding it*). Each shows what the others hide.

**Ground / Figure / Pattern (GFP).** The three-role structure found inside every axis: ground is the enabling condition, figure is the differentiated element, pattern is the stabilizing integration. A second-order observation about the axes, not what generates them. See [Ground / Figure / Pattern](/ground-figure-pattern).

**Triadic Minimum.** The principle that three elements, not two, are the smallest structure that sustains stable differentiation and transformation. See [The Triadic Minimum](/the-triadic-minimum).

**Khora.** The unnameable generative ground prior to all structure (Plato's "third kind" in the *Timaeus*). EO acknowledges it and does not claim to formalize it. See [The Khora, The Substate, and the 10,000 Things](/the-khora-the-substate-and-the-10000-things).

**Substrate.** EO's formalized structure: the helical morphospace of 27 transformation types with its dependency ordering. Co-emergent with the Khora, not a higher or lower level of reality.

## The nine operators

Atomic kinds of change, claimed to suffice to describe any transformation. Best read as **capacities** a system becomes able to do, not steps it executes. See [The Nine Operators](/the-nine-operators).

| Operator | Glyph | Triad | Role | Capacity |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **NUL** | ∅ | Existence | Ground | Not transform: the identity function on state. See [NUL](/nul). |
| **SIG** | ○ | Existence | Figure | Register an appearance: attention without enduring structure. See [SIG](/sig). |
| **INS** | ● | Existence | Pattern | Create a concrete instance: that a thing *is*, not what it is. See [INS](/ins). |
| **SEG** | ｜ | Structure | Ground | Draw boundaries. Also the fundamental query operator. |
| **CON** | ⋈ | Structure | Figure | Establish relationships between differentiated elements. |
| **SYN** | △ | Structure | Pattern | Produce emergent wholes. |
| **DEF** | ⊢ | Significance | Ground | Establish what holds within a stable interpretive frame. |
| **EVA** | ⊨ | Significance | Figure | Render judgment by testing a particular against a general. |
| **REC** | ⊛ | Significance | Pattern | Restructure the frame itself. See [REC](/rec). |

**Triad.** A group of three operators that co-arise as one transformation within a domain: Existence (NUL, SIG, INS), Structure (SEG, CON, SYN), Significance (DEF, EVA, REC). The triad, not the single operator, is the smallest complete unit of domain-level change. See [The Three Triads](/the-triads).

**Helix.** The dependency ordering of the nine capacities: NUL → SIG → INS → SEG → CON → SYN → DEF → EVA → REC. It is an order of enablement, not a recipe. See [The Dependency Ordering](/the-dependency-ordering).

**DEF → EVA → REC loop.** The Significance cycle: assert what holds, test particulars against it, restructure the frame when testing demands. See [The DEF → EVA → REC Loop](/the-def-eva-rec-loop).

**Decal.** A mark (−, +, \*) appended to an operator to pin its Object coordinate: Ground, Figure, Pattern. EVA− judges an ambient condition, EVA+ a specific particular, EVA\* a regularity. An operator written without a decal is unmarked. See [Decal Notation](/decal-notation).

**Targeting and scoping.** Targeting is how a target is identified (nine reference types); scoping is how broad the operation is. See [Targeting and Scoping](/scope).

**Site** (terrain). One of nine places an act can land: Void, Entity, Kind, Field, Link, Network, Atmosphere, Lens, Paradigm. See [The Nine Operators](/the-nine-operators).

## Entity types

Three configurations describing how phenomena manifest, each defined by which of Figure, Pattern, Ground dominates.

**Emanon** (∞×). Ground-dominant: ambient, contextual, proliferating under direct examination. "Too vague." The word is both singular and plural. See [Emanon](/emanon).

**Protogon.** Figure-dominant: identity actively crystallizing but not yet self-maintaining. "Too unstable." See [Protogon](/protogon).

**Holon** (∞↻). Pattern-dominant: a self-sustaining recursive process that binds figure and ground. "Too rigid." Redefined from Koestler by configuration, not by place in a hierarchy. See [Holons](/holons).

**Measurement Paradox.** Recording an emanonic or protogonic entity forces it into holon-grade, because INS mints holon-grade anchors only. A forced dropdown at intake is an instance. See [The Measurement Paradox](/the-measurement-paraxoc).

## Identity and definition

**Identity.** What a thing is *for someone*, not what it is in itself: the neighborhood the reading connects to it, cut off where the difference stops mattering, seen from a perspective. Relative (to a for-whom), revisable, and earned. The identity definition is still a nomination in the code, with its measurements pending. See [Identity and Definition](/identity-and-definition).

**Definition.** The same fold written down in a portable phrase, by someone, so it can be carried and reused. It cannot replace the word; it shifts the odds of later usage. The claim that a definition acts as a prior on usage is untested. See [Identity and Definition](/identity-and-definition).

**For-whom.** The perspective from which a fold is cut. It is the giver of a definition, which is why a definition is only as authoritative as its giver.

**Log identity.** The view that an entity is its history, not its current properties. EO commits to it. See [Identity as a Change Log](/identity-as-a-change-log).

## The Experience Engine

**Experience Engine.** EO's applied architecture: a Given-Log, a Structure-Lattice, a Meant-Graph, and three functions (provenance, availability, supersession). See [The Experience Engine](/the-experience-engine).

**Given-Log.** The append-only record of what was observed. Never edited or deleted. Holds the seven emitting operators, INS through REC. NUL and SIG produce no entries.

**Structure-Lattice.** The partially ordered set of relationships among Given-Log entries; it also defines where an observer can stand.

**Meant-Graph.** What the record means: the interpretation layer.

**Horizon.** The projection: an observation of a query against the three stores from a position in the lattice. It writes nothing. There is no unmediated access.

**The bar.** The boundary that keeps projections from becoming record. Nothing crosses it leftward except emission, and emission is reserved for what was given, not inferred. A wrong projection is a wrong reading, not a corrupted record.

**Integral model.** State computed as the integral (accumulation) of an append-only log of typed transformations, rather than stored and updated in place. A database built this way is an Experience Engine. See [The Integral Model](/the-integral-model).

## The EO Reader

The EO Reader is the framework running as software. See [The EO Reader](/the-eo-reader).

**Projection.** Everything shown is a recomputed view of the event log. Re-projecting the same log is byte-identical, and nothing derived is persisted.

**Door** (perceiver / enactor). Every event enters the log through one of two doors. The perceiver door carries the witnessed world (exafference); the enactor door carries the system's own output (reafference). See [The Two Doors](/the-two-doors).

**canWitness.** Whether something can ground a claim. True for the perceiver door and false for the enactor door, by type and not by flag, so the system's own reasoning cannot be laundered into evidence for itself.

**Derived void-null.** A cut derived from the material's own chance background instead of a hand-set threshold. When meaning cannot be measured, abstention is the honest output. See [Signal from Noise](/signal-from-noise).

**Surprise.** The single surprise metric the reader uses: Bayesian surprise (a KL divergence from prior to posterior), deliberately not surprisal. Computed once and read through two channels.

**Deep reading.** What the machine does at rest: surf to the place of most interest, fold the passage, reflect. Its reflections are interpretations and never enter the record. Habituation prevents rumination. See [Deep Reading](/deep-reading).

**Disproof stance.** Seeding a share of research searches to falsify the emerging reading rather than confirm it. See [Going and Looking](/going-and-looking).

**Model contract.** The narrow grant of authority given to the language model: no DEF, EVA, or REC, and no Entity terrain. "The model proposes, the kernel disposes." See [The Model Is the Leaf](/the-model-is-the-leaf).

**EOT** (EO Text). The EO notation as a working wire format. Each line carries its operator in its punctuation, so the operator is recovered from the surface and not inferred. See [EOT](/eot) and [EO Notation](/eo-notation).

## Terms retired or not to be confused

**Formerly DEF / EVA (Alternation / Superposition).** Older articles used these names for DEF and EVA. The current meanings are those in the table above.

**Void, Clearing, NUL.** Three different things. NUL is an operator, Void is a Site, Clearing is a Stance.

**Distinction.** SIG's former name, retired because distinction is a Differentiating act that cuts this from that, while SIG sits at Relating and registers. Drawing boundaries is SEG's job: SIG registers, SEG bounds. See [SIG](/sig) and [Operator Naming](/operator-naming-in-emergent-ontology).

**CLM (Claim).** Not a tenth operator: SIG performed after REC, with the full cycle's context available. See the [EO Handbook](/eo-handbook).
